Our Services

Medical Departments

Explore the specialist healthcare services available at Habib Hospital.

Dermatology

Diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the skin, hair and nails, including allergies, infections and chronic skin disorders.

General Surgery

Surgical consultation and treatment for a range of conditions, including abdominal, soft-tissue and other general surgical problems.

Gastroenterology

Evaluation and management of digestive-system conditions involving the stomach, intestines, liver, gallbladder and related organs.

Pediatrics

Medical care for infants, children and adolescents, including routine assessment, illness management and preventive care.

Gynaecology

Women's healthcare covering reproductive health, menstrual concerns, pregnancy-related consultations and other gynaecological conditions.

General Medicine

Comprehensive assessment, diagnosis and treatment of common adult medical conditions and ongoing health concerns.

Orthopaedics

Care for bone, joint and musculoskeletal problems, including fractures, arthritis, injuries and mobility-related conditions.

Radiology

Diagnostic imaging services that support medical assessment, diagnosis and treatment planning across hospital departments.

Laboratory Services

Clinical laboratory testing of blood and other samples to support diagnosis, health screening and treatment monitoring.

Physiotherapy

Rehabilitation and movement therapy to improve strength, mobility and function following injury, surgery or illness.

Nutrition & Dietetics

Nutrition assessment and personalised dietary guidance to support general health and the management of medical conditions.

Pain Management

Assessment and management of acute and chronic pain using appropriate medical and supportive treatment approaches.

Rheumatology

Diagnosis and management of arthritis, autoimmune disorders and other conditions affecting joints, muscles and connective tissues.
